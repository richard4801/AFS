Werewolf Romance Writing Jobs

Apex Fiction Studio commissions writers to create serialised werewolf and paranormal romance for readers on leading fiction platforms. If you can write a fast, emotional story with a pack, a mate bond and a twist at the end of every chapter, this page explains how the work and the pay are set up.

What we commission

Werewolf romance is the core of what we publish. It is one of the highest-earning genres in serialised fiction, and readers return to it daily. We also commission closely related commercial paranormal romance.

The stories that work best on our platforms usually have:

  • A clear romantic lead and a strong alpha, Luna or mate-bond dynamic
  • Pack politics, rejection, rivalry or a hidden identity driving the plot
  • Short, fast chapters that each end on a reason to keep reading
  • A long arc that can run for many chapters without losing pace

You don't need a publishing history. We judge the first three chapters of your work, not your CV.

How you get paid

Every writer chooses one of two pay tracks when they sign.

Shared revenue track

You receive 50% of the gross royalties your book earns, every month, for the life of the five-year contract. The more readers your book holds, the more you earn.

Flat rate track

You're paid ₦2 per word each month, with no royalty share. It suits writers who want predictable income. You have 30 days after signing to switch tracks.

Both tracks include a milestone bonus when your first book passes 30,000 words. Full details are on our Terms of Partnership page.

What we handle

You write. We provide editing, plagiarism screening, distribution and the platform relationship. An Executive Editor works with you on plot, pacing and chapter structure for the full contract. You also get a private dashboard with daily earnings and live reader comments.

Our rules

  • Human writing only. AI-generated or plagiarised work ends the contract.
  • Pen names. You publish under a pen name agreed with us, and your real identity stays private.

How to apply

Send us the first three chapters of any story you've written, plus a one-sentence logline for the book you'd like to write. We read every application personally and reply within 72 hours.
